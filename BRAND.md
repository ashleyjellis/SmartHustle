# Brand Writing Principles

## Three principles
- **Straightforward kindness** – clear, inclusive, focused on the reader. Always applies.
- **Everyday magic** – elevate the mundane with unexpected delight. Brand/marketing mainly.
- **Warm wit** – humour as seasoning, never the main course. Brand/social mainly.

## Voice rules
- Swap formal words for normal ones (Assistance→Help, Require→Need, Utilise→Use)
- Active voice, not passive ("We've declined your application", not "A decision has been made")
- Say sorry sincerely — once — then focus on fixing it
- No empty adjectives (great, simple, easy, quick)
- No marketing clichés (simply, discover, and much more)
- Humour: smart asides, not cheap puns. Never punch down.

## Channel tone levels (Straightforward kindness / Everyday magic / Warm wit)
- Operational comms: Max / Low / None
- Customer service: High / Low / None
- Marketing: High / Medium / Medium
- Campaigns: High / High / High
- Organic social: High / High / Max

## Visual brand
- Primary coral/red: #FF3F34
- Dark navy: #1A1A2E
- Background light: #F8F8FA
- Clean sans-serif typography (Inter preferred)
